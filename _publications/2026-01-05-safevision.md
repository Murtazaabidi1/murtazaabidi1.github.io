A unified vision–language framework for real-time, context-aware safety assessment. A frozen CLIP-ViT encoder is combined with a Scene-Aware Reasoner (SAR) for global context and a Region-Focused Neural Reasoner (ReFiNER) for region-level compliance verification, with parameter-efficient LoRA adaptation.
